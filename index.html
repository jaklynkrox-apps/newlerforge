Newlerforge


NewlerForge is a version of Forge for Eaglercraft, but what makes us different is our implementation. While other platforms make an entirely different mod loading system that is then injected into your Eaglercraft copy, with us you can put in any Eaglercraft client of the proper Minecraft version and Forge will run alongside it! With this you can play mods on clients that Eaglerforge cannot inject into, and can mix Eaglerforge and Forge mods together.
